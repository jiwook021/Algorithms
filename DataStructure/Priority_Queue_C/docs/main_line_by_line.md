### **Heap Data Structure and Operations**

#### What Is a Heap?
A **heap** is a specialized tree-based data structure that satisfies the **heap property**:
- In a **max-heap**, the parent node is always greater than or equal to its children.
- In a **min-heap**, the parent node is always less than or equal to its children.

#### Why Use a Heap?
- Heaps allow efficient insertion and deletion of elements while maintaining order.
- The highest-priority element is always at the root, making it easy to access.

#### Heap Operations:
1. **Insertion (`HInsert`)**:
   - Adds a new element to the heap.
   - The element is placed at the bottom of the heap and "bubbled up" to its correct position.

2. **Deletion (`Hdelete`)**:
   - Removes the root element (highest priority).
   - The last element in the heap is moved to the root and "bubbled down" to its correct position.
